# Smart-Logistics-Analytics-End-to-End-Data-Analysis-Prediction
“Built an end-to-end logistics analytics system combining EDA, machine learning, and anomaly detection to uncover delay drivers and improve operational efficiency.”


## 📌Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of a smart logistics dataset, combining descriptive analytics and machine learning to uncover operational inefficiencies and predict shipment delays.

The analysis reveals key performance bottlenecks across logistics operations, with a focus on delay patterns, traffic conditions, asset performance, and environmental factors.

---

## 🎯Project Objectives

- Analyze logistics performance across 2024  
- Identify key drivers of shipment delays  
- Evaluate the impact of traffic and environmental conditions  
- Build predictive models for delay detection  
- Detect anomalies and uncover root causes  

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

- **Dataset:** Smart Logistics Dataset  
- **Records:** ~1,000 shipments  

**Features include:**
- Shipment status  
- Traffic conditions  
- Waiting time  
- Environmental data (IoT sensors)  
- Delay reasons  
- Geographic and transactional data

### 3. Predictive Modeling

- **Root Cause Analysis:** Understand delay drivers  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- A significant proportion of shipments experience delays, indicating systemic inefficiencies  
- Traffic conditions are a major contributor to delays  
- Waiting time strongly correlates with delay occurrence  
- Certain assets consistently underperform, suggesting maintenance or allocation issues  
- Environmental factors (e.g., temperature, humidity) influence logistics reliability
